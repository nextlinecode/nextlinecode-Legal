Privacy Policy

monoWeather displays weather and air-quality information for your current location or a place you choose. This draft describes information that may be processed when you use the app and the choices available to you.

Information and purposes

  • Location and places: If you grant location permission, the app uses your device location to show nearby weather. You can also search for and choose places. Coordinates of a selected place may be sent to OpenWeather and WAQI to obtain weather and air quality. OpenStreetMap Nominatim or your operating system's location services may be used to identify a place; search terms may be sent to Nominatim.
  • Information stored on your device: Saved places, the last location, purchase entitlement and some settings may be stored locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove local information, depending on your device and operating system settings.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: Google Firebase Analytics, Remote Config and Crashlytics may process installation, device, technical usage and event information for analytics, app configuration and diagnostics. App-authored analytics events are designed not to include exact coordinates, place names, search terms or purchase tokens. Google Privacy Policy
  • Advertising: The free version displays advertising through Google AdMob. Advertising providers may process device and app information, IP address, ad interactions and applicable consent choices for advertising and measurement. The app requests non-personalized ads. Where available, you can review or change advertising privacy choices. Unity Ads mediation is included in the app, but the corresponding production mediation groups are currently paused. If activated, Unity may process information needed to provide and measure ads. Google Privacy Policy · Unity Privacy Policy
  • Purchases: Remove Ads purchases and restoration use Apple App Store or Google Play billing. The app checks product and entitlement status; it does not directly receive complete payment card numbers.

Retention, deletion and providers

Local information may be removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to your device and operating system settings. Information held separately by Apple, Google or other providers may be retained under their own policies and settings and may not be deleted automatically when the app is removed. Service providers may process information in other countries as part of their operations. Please consult each provider’s privacy policy for its processing and retention practices.

Your choices and contact

You may change location permissions in your device settings and can use selected places without allowing current-location access. Where available, the app's ad privacy settings allow you to change advertising choices. You may contact us about access, correction or deletion of personal information.
